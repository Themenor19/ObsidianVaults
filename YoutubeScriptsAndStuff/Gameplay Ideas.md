
# Crafting

#### Spells are made of essences
As the title suggests. Spells are made out of essences. For example, the fireflower might give 1-4 fire essences, and you need 2 fire essences to make 1 basic fireball. And the more essences you use, the more powerful the spell. For example, a tier II fireball could require 8 essences, and a tier III would require 25 essences or something, And you could maybe get buffs that reduce the amount of essences needed to by 20% or something.
